Workshop at EMNLP 2026

Grounding Language Models: Learning Faithfully and Efficiently

GroundLM 2026 brings together work on grounding, faithfulness, efficiency, hallucination mitigation, retrieval, evaluation, and real-world reliability.

October 29, 2026 EMNLP 2026 Workshop Budapest, Hungary
Call For Papers

What Papers We Welcome

We invite original research on grounded language models, especially work that improves faithful behavior, efficient adaptation, evidence use, and evaluation in realistic settings.

Faithful grounding, hallucination reduction, attribution, and verifiability
Efficient grounding, PEFT, LoRA, prompt tuning, feedback, and scaling
Retrieval, external knowledge, tool use, interaction, action, and embodiment
Multimodal grounding, benchmarks, metrics, robustness, privacy, and bias
